Frequency estimation & search

Area-constrained frequency optimization

Velocities are searched while middle-phase rheology remains fixed. Qualified candidates meet both area tolerances, descriptor bounds and support constraints. Frequency modes also require agreement between the phase-specific estimates.

Qᵢ = vᵢw; Qₘ = 2vₘw; fᵢ = Qᵢ/Aᵢ; fₘ = Qₘ/Aₘ. The common estimate is (Qᵢ + Qₘ)/(Aᵢ + Aₘ), reported only when 200|fᵢ − fₘ|/(fᵢ + fₘ) meets the selected tolerance. Areas are converted to m² internally.

Frequency is estimated from 2D phase conservation for periodic single-core formation, equal 50 μm inlets, and negligible accumulation or bypass. It is not a directly measured detachment frequency.

What the search establishes

Deterministic Sobol sampling (16,384 points plus known-condition seeds) and up to 10 constrained SLSQP refinements identify candidates. Results are the best feasible candidates identified by the search, not a certificate of a global physical maximum. New designs require independent numerical or experimental verification.

387 stable cases define proximity; 133 nonstable observations provide an optional exclusion reference. Neither proximity nor exclusion guarantees the formation regime at a new condition.
